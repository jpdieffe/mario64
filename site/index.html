Two visible characters in the same world. Each player moves independently with their own camera.

Land on their head to bounce. Ask them to crouch for an extra boost. Friendly attacks won’t hurt them.

03 / TRY AGAIN

Nobody gets left behind.

A fall or an empty health bar restarts both players at the failed course’s entrance. Your stars stay collected.
